WELCOME TO EMBASSY

A church family.
A world-changing faith.

We believe faith is not meant to end at the church doors. It should shape how we live, love, lead, serve, and influence the world around us.

Embassy of Faith Worldwide is a Christian church in Nairobi, Kenya. We invite you to join us as we worship God, learn from Scripture, build meaningful relationships, and grow in the purpose He has placed before us.

Whether you are visiting for the first time, returning to church, or looking for a place to grow spiritually, we would be glad to welcome you.
